About The 3rd Set

Our story

If you’ve ever tried finding good writing or coverage about D3 tennis, it’s pretty much non-existent. There’s no real conversation. No one breaks down what truly happens or why it matters.

That’s why we started this.

We’re two people who love D3 tennis — we played it, watched it, and argued with each other way too late at night about it. We got tired of there not being a community to talk about it seriously. Not ESPN-level seriousness: just two friends who care about the sport we play and want to build an open community around it.

Over time, we want to attract a community that’s as fascinated by this sport as we are. If that sounds like your kind of thing, stick around.

What we cover

  • Recaps & analysis of the big matches
  • Rankings and awards — including where the ITA gets it wrong
  • Conference shake-ups and coaching moves
  • The culture and stories of D3 tennis
  • Predictions, debates, and whatever feels right
